# CodeCortex provenance

The public product identity is CodeCortex. Required inherited and third-party provenance is maintained only in the licensing surfaces that need it: `LEGAL/UPSTREAM_ATTRIBUTION.md`, `NOTICE`, `LICENSES/`, `THIRD_PARTY.md`, `third_party/`, and applicable inherited source headers.

Historical development-session and benchmark lineage artifacts are intentionally excluded from the clean public repository.
